Wardi Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Wardi is a dhikr counter published by Abdullahi Farah Abdigaani under the brand Ibadurahman. This policy explains what Wardi does with your information. In short, Wardi collects nothing.

What Wardi does not do

  • It does not record or save audio.
  • It does not send audio, text or counts to any server.
  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not contain ads, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share any data.

Microphone and speech recognition

Wardi asks for microphone and speech recognition permission so it can hear your dhikr and count it. Recognition runs on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is processed as it is heard and is discarded immediately. If on-device recognition is not available for your device or language, Wardi does not listen.

Information stored on your device

Wardi stores the following only on your device: your daily counts, your goals, any custom dhikr you add, your Qur'an page counts, and your app settings. This information is used to show your progress, streaks and insights. It never leaves your device through Wardi. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it. You can also erase it at any time from Profile, Delete all data.

Notifications

Wardi may show a notification while it is listening so you can see that the microphone is in use. It does not send marketing notifications.

Children

Wardi does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
